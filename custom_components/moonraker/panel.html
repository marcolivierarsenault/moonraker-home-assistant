GUIDED TRAMMING — adjust while watching live

Each corner shows its height above the lowest. Lower the high corners (or raise the rest) by that amount. Turn estimates assume common 0.5mm and 0.75mm screw pitches — run a fresh mesh after each pass and watch the offsets converge to zero.
